# Third-party visual references

The portfolio distinguishes personal contributions from published research and public product examples.

## NeuRAD actor relocation

The actor-shift image is a detail of Figure 1 from *NeuRAD: Neural Rendering for Autonomous Driving*, Tonderski et al., CVPR 2024. It was supplied with the internship report and visually matched to the published figure. The site labels it a research reference rather than a personal experimental result. The original figure identifies the source scene as nuScenes.

- [Project and authors](https://research.zenseact.com/publications/neurad/)
- [Original full figure](https://research.zenseact.com/publications/neurad/frontfig.png)
- [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.15260)

## FiftyOne by Voxel51

The projection-grid animation is an unmodified official FiftyOne documentation example. It is labeled a public tool reference, not a capture of workplace data. Playback requires a visitor action.

- [Official documentation](https://docs.voxel51.com/user_guide/app.html#using-the-3d-visualizer)
- [Original asset](https://docs.voxel51.com/_images/app-orthographic-projections.gif)
- [Repository source](https://github.com/voxel51/fiftyone/blob/main/docs/source/images/app/app-orthographic-projections.gif)
- [Repository license](https://github.com/voxel51/fiftyone/blob/main/LICENSE), copied in `public/assets/fiftyone-LICENSE.txt`

The software license does not establish authorship of the dataset imagery embedded in the public demonstration. Visible attribution identifies Voxel51 as the source.
